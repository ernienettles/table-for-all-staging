The Reality

A Crisis in Plain Sight

Pedregal Grande sits roughly 3,000 miles from the United States — and in many ways, it feels even further. The community has no healthcare, no running water, inconsistent electricity, and no basic sanitation. Children suffer from malnutrition — no access to fresh vegetables, and dairy like milk and cheese is a luxury most families cannot afford.

Water contamination with hepatitis A and parasites is common. The community lacked a playground, proper latrines, or any infrastructure that most of us take for granted.

Yet despite all of this, the people are warmhearted — quick with a smile, full of hope. The question that drove our team was simple: How can we help in a way that lasts?

Programs That Are Changing Lives

Blood Bread: Fighting Anemia

Our "blood bread" — iron-enriched bread made by two young men, Summer and Van — is being distributed to 70 children from the neighborhood, 5 days a week for 90 days. Hemoglobin tests establish a baseline before the program begins, with retesting after 90 days to measure improvement. This is medicine delivered through bread.

Protein Meals for Families

As of August 10, families in the area are receiving protein meals three times per week. This consistent access to nourishing food is making a measurable difference in a community where hunger is the default condition of daily life. Our team has walked miles to deliver these meals and see the impact firsthand.

Those Who Need Us Most

Three Elderly Ladies

In Pedregal Grande, three elderly ladies — in their 70s and 80s — have no family to care for them. They are entirely dependent on neighbors for basic needs. Table for All is committed to ensuring these vulnerable neighbors are not forgotten. No one should face old age alone.

Vacation Bible School 2025

Nearly 225 children from the surrounding community participated in our Vacation Bible School — a partnership with Olive Branch Ministries. For four days, children experienced puppet lessons, singing, sports camp, and the simple message that they are loved. Many received their first hot, nutritious meal in a long time.

In this place, Table for All is making a difference. It's just a great seed that's been sewn.
