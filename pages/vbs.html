JULY 18–21, 2026

Vacation Bible School 2026

Four days of joy, learning, and community on the Table for All farm in Pedregal Grande, Peru.

What to Expect

A Week of Wonder

Vacation Bible School 2026 will take place July 18–21 at the Table for All farm in Pedregal Grande, Peru. Partnering with Olive Branch Ministries, we will welcome 185 children from the surrounding community alongside 25 adult volunteers for four transformative days.

Each day includes four teaching stations featuring puppet lessons, singing, Bible-based activities, and sports camp — including soccer and other recreational activities. Every child receives a daily nutritious hot meal, and the older children will have the opportunity to participate in bread baking sessions — learning skills they can carry with them for life.
