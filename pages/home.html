Our Mission

Everyone Deserves a Seat at the Table

At Table for All, we are dedicated to improving the lives of individuals and families in Northern Peru who face daily challenges in meeting basic needs. We believe that everyone deserves access to clean water, nourishing food, safe and sustainable living conditions, and quality healthcare.

Through community-driven initiatives and long-term solutions, we work to create lasting change — empowering communities, restoring dignity, and building a healthier, more hopeful future for those we serve.

What We Are Doing

Growing Hope on the Farm

Goat Milk & Dairy

Growing from 5 to 30 goats, our farm produces nutritious goat milk, cheese, and butter — addressing protein deficiency in children and elderly alike.

Blood Bread Program

Our iron-enriched "blood bread" is changing lives. 70 children receive it 5 days a week for 90 days, with hemoglobin testing before and after.

Protein Meals

Families in the Pedregal Grande region now receive protein meals three times per week — a critical intervention in a community where food insecurity is the norm.
