Our History

Where It All Began

Table for All is a nonprofit organization focused on serving communities in Northern Peru. Its roots began in 2023 during a medical mission trip to the Pedregal Grande region, sponsored by Olive Branch Ministries.

One evening, a small group of healthcare professionals and business leaders gathered with a simple but powerful question: How can we create a lasting impact in this community? That conversation became the foundation for something much greater.

What followed was a dedicated effort to establish Table for All as a nonprofit organization in both the United States and Peru. Through extensive legal work, planning, and a shared commitment to sustainable change, the vision began to take shape. A key milestone was securing land for a community farm — an initiative designed to address both immediate and long-term needs.

Today, the Table for All farm plays a vital role in supporting the community. It provides meals for those facing hunger, produces fresh bread to help combat anemia, and supplies nutritious goat milk to improve overall health. From a single conversation to a growing movement, Table for All continues to build hope and opportunity for the communities it serves.

Our Partners

Working Together

GS

Global Samaritan Resources

Founded in 1999, this 501(c)(3) organization operates a 55,000 sq ft warehouse in Abilene, Texas, delivering aid to 56+ countries. They provide water purification systems, disaster relief, medical supplies, and educational materials — serving as our logistics hub.

TH

Global Health (Texas Tech)

Texas Tech University medical brigades have implemented community-based composting latrines, trained community health agent groups, and conducted water quality testing. Their capacity-building approach strengthens local systems rather than replacing them.

OB

Olive Branch Ministries

U.S.-based Christian nonprofit with 25+ years in Peru. Coordinates short-term medical brigades — general medicine, dental, and vision. Partners with Table for All for VBS programs, medical campaigns, and ongoing nutritional follow-up in Pedregal, Piura.
