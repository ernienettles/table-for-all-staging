Here's What Your Gift Does

Every dollar goes directly to feeding families, growing the farm, and improving health outcomes in Pedregal Grande.

$25

Provides one month of iron-enriched "blood bread" for a child suffering from anemia — 5 days a week for 4 weeks.

$50

Supplies protein meals for an entire family for one full week — three nutritious meals, three times that week.

$100

Funds the training of a community health agent — a local resident equipped with first aid, CPR, and health prevention knowledge.

$250

Supplies goat feed for one full month — helping our dairy goat herd grow from 5 to 30 and produce milk, cheese, and butter.
