### Our Mission
At Table for All, we are dedicated to improving the lives of individuals and families in Northern Peru who face daily challenges in meeting basic needs. We believe that everyone deserves access to clean water, nourishing food, safe and sustainable living conditions, and quality healthcare.
Through community-driven initiatives and long-term solutions, we work to create lasting change—empowering communities, restoring dignity, and building a healthier, more hopeful future for those we serve.

### Our History
Table for All is a nonprofit organization focused on serving communities in Northern Peru. Its roots began in 2023 during a medical mission trip to the Pedregal Grande region, sponsored by Olive Branch Ministries.
One evening, a small group of healthcare professionals and business leaders gathered with a simple but powerful question: How can we create a lasting impact in this community? That conversation became the foundation for something much greater.
What followed was a dedicated effort to establish Table for All as a nonprofit organization in both the United States and Peru. Through extensive legal work, planning, and a shared commitment to sustainable change, the vision began to take shape. A key milestone was securing land for a community farm—an initiative designed to address both immediate and long-term needs.
Today, the Table for All farm plays a vital role in supporting the community. It provides meals for those facing hunger, produces fresh bread to help combat anemia, and supplies nutritious goat milk to improve overall health.
From a single conversation to a growing movement, Table for All continues to build hope and opportunity for the communities it serves.

### Rural Healthcare & Community Impact in Pedregal Chico, Peru
Rural healthcare in Pedregal Chico, Peru, is being transformed through holistic, community-driven initiatives like the Table for All farm. By addressing both physical and spiritual needs, this effort is helping build a healthier, more connected community.
Through hands-on outreach—including home visits, meal deliveries, iron-rich bread distribution, and community health fairs—the Table for All team has cultivated strong, trusting relationships with residents, healthcare providers, and local leaders. These consistent, personal interactions not only improve access to nutrition and care, but also foster dignity and trust.
Beyond combating food insecurity and promoting better nutrition, Table for All creates meaningful opportunities for spiritual growth and connection. One powerful example is its partnership with Olive Branch Ministries International, which helped host nearly 225 children for Vacation Bible School last summer—offering encouragement, joy, and a sense of belonging.
This integrated approach—meeting practical needs while nurturing spiritual well-being—is strengthening health outcomes, inspiring hope, and contributing to the long-term flourishing of the Pedregal Chico community.

### Water Condition Data (OGH Peru Program, July 2025)

- 85% contaminated with coliform bacteria (unsafe water)
- Communities: Narihualá (83.3%), La Campiña (88.2%), and others
- Most households use piped water but drink untreated
- Key need: water infrastructure, treatment practices, community education
